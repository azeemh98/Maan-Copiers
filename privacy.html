Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

MAAN COPIERS ("we", "us") runs a stock and business management app for its print and copy shop in Lucknow, India. This page explains what information the app stores and how it is used. The app is for our staff and authorised users only.

Information we store

  • Staff accounts: username, login email, role (for example admin, operator, walk-in), and a securely hashed password. We never see or store passwords in readable form.
  • Business records: job tickets, invoices, payments, expenses, stock movements and party (customer and supplier) ledgers.
  • Customer details entered by staff: names, phone numbers, GST details and addresses that appear on tickets and invoices.
  • Employee records: attendance and salary payments, visible to admins only.

How we use it

Only to run the shop: creating tickets and invoices, tracking stock and dues, producing GST and accounting exports, and keeping staff access secure. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) in the Mumbai region, and the app is hosted on Vercel. Access to each shop's data is restricted to signed-in members of that shop through database row-level security.

Usage statistics

The app may collect anonymous page-view statistics (no cookies, no personal identifiers) to understand which screens are used and to fix errors.

Who can see your data

Only authorised staff according to their role. Service providers named above process data on our behalf to operate the app. We share records with accountants or authorities only when needed for tax or legal compliance.

Retention and deletion

Business records are kept for as long as needed for accounting and GST compliance. If you are a customer and want your contact details corrected or removed from our records, or a staff member who wants their account removed, ask the shop administrator and we will act on it, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

Staff can change their password from the top bar at any time. Admins can issue password-reset emails and remove accounts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, speak to the shop administrator at MAAN COPIERS, Lucknow.
