Net Position
—
This is Cash & Bank + Receivable − Payable as of the chosen date — not a full balance sheet. It doesn't include an inventory valuation (stock is tracked by quantity, not cost, so there's no rupee figure to include), fixed assets (machines, furniture — not tracked at all), or a capital/equity account. Useful as a quick internal check; not something to hand a CA as a substitute for a proper statutory balance sheet.

Tally Export — voucher entries for the period selected above

A day-book style Excel sheet — one row per ledger line, grouped by voucher number, Debit and Credit in separate columns (no sign convention to get wrong). Covers Sales, Purchases, Payments Received/Made, and Expenses. Hand this to your accountant, or key it into Tally directly.
